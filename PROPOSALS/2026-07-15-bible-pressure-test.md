# Bible pressure test — July 2026

The last pressure test (2026-05-17, still AWAITING_REVIEW) flagged the six-engine gap and the budget/enterprise bifurcation. Those haven't been actioned yet and still stand. This round surfaces three new developments from the last two months that point to different edits — not a repeat of May's.

## 1. What changed in the market

- **Peec AI's growth accelerated further.** It's now in talks for a $200M valuation (up from the ~$155M implied by its Nov 2025 Series A), hit $10M ARR just 16 months after launch — doubling from $5M in five months — opened a New York office, and assembled a dedicated research team to study why LLMs recommend some brands over others. ([TechCrunch, May 23 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/23/peec-one-of-berlins-rising-startups-more-than-doubled-annualized-revenue-in-months-to-10m-sources-say/); [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/peec-ai-fundraise-new-valuation); [GlobeNewswire, Jun 26 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/26/3318325/0/en/Peec-AI-the-AI-visibility-monitoring-company-assembles-a-team-to-decode-how-ChatGPT-and-other-LLMs-recommend-brands.html))
- **Profound moved from monitoring into execution.** At its "Zero Click New York" event it launched the Profound Index — a daily-updated public benchmark across 1.5B+ prompts and 50+ industries — and Profound Aim, an "always-on background agent" that identifies opportunities and coordinates marketing execution, not just reports on visibility. ([GlobeNewswire, Jun 29 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/29/3319173/0/en/profound-launches-the-profound-index-at-zero-click-new-york-creating-the-definitive-benchmark-for-ai-search-visibility.html); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/media-advertising/articles/profound-launches-ai-transform-ai-130000823.html))
- **Share-of-voice is now a marketing weapon, and it's volatile.** A published 30-day comparative study has AthenaHQ (a much smaller player — $2.7M total raised) gaining 45% answer share against Peec's 8% and a reported decline for Profound. Whether or not the methodology holds up, this is the second independent "who's winning" benchmark to appear in two months (after Profound's own Index). ([GetMint AthenaHQ review](https://getmint.ai/resources/athenahq-review))
- **The strongest headline stat yet for "why GEO matters" has firmed up.** ~68% of Google searches ended without a click between January and April 2026, per SparkToro's analysis of Similarweb clickstream data — up sharply year over year. This is more concrete and more current than anything the bible or site copy currently cites. ([via Frase.io roundup, 2026](https://www.frase.io/blog/the-10-best-ai-visibility-tools-in-2026))
- **Buyer expectations on price have drifted up, and bundling is a new competitive vector.** Multiple 2026 buyer's-guide roundups now describe $99–$299/mo as the expected range for early-stage-SaaS-grade GEO tooling. Separately, "all-in-one" entrants like Frase are pairing AI-engine tracking with content research/writing/publishing in a single workflow, rather than staying in pure-monitoring lane. ([Frase.io](https://www.frase.io/blog/the-10-best-ai-visibility-tools-in-2026); [PallasAI](https://www.pallasai.io/blog/best-geo-platforms-for-early-stage-saas-teams-2026))

## 2. Where the bible may need updating

### Section 1 (Identity) — draw a boundary against the "execution agent" trend

Before:

> **What we are not:** We are not a full-stack SEO platform. We are not a content writer. We are not a website builder. We are not an agency. We do one narrow thing and we do it well: continuous AI search visibility monitoring for small business websites.

After:

> **What we are not:** We are not a full-stack SEO platform. We are not a content writer. We are not a website builder. We are not an agency. We are not an always-on execution agent — Profound's move into agentic marketing coordination (its "Aim" product) is a different product for a different buyer with a different budget. We do one narrow thing and we do it well: tell a small business owner where they stand in AI search and what to fix. They still do the fixing.

Rationale: as the enterprise tier moves from "report on visibility" to "coordinate execution for you," Olokas's narrow-scope positioning needs an explicit line, or the "what we are not" list will read as outdated the next time someone compares us to Profound.

### Section 5 (Acquisition) — require a current, sourced stat in cornerstone content

Before:

> **Content cadence:** 3 articles per week, minimum 1,800 words each, all originally written (not regurgitated), all with a clear actionable takeaway. We are competing on quality and freshness, not volume.

After:

> **Content cadence:** 3 articles per week, minimum 1,800 words each, all originally written (not regurgitated), all with a clear actionable takeaway. We are competing on quality and freshness, not volume. Every cornerstone piece (the pillar "why GEO matters" page, comparison pages, and anything making a market-size argument) must cite a current zero-click stat — currently: ~68% of Google searches ended without a click, Jan–Apr 2026 (SparkToro/Similarweb) — instead of vague "AI search is growing" framing. This stat has a shelf life; re-verify each quarter before reusing it.

Rationale: this is the most concrete, third-party-sourced number available right now for justifying the product's existence. It's stronger than anything currently in the bible's own copy examples (Section 4).

### Section 5 (Acquisition), Channel 2 — add a scan-volume-gated PR idea

Before:

> ### Channel 2 — Free tool as lead magnet (second priority)
> The single-domain free scan is our most powerful acquisition tool. It needs to:
> - Be embeddable (offer agencies an embed code in exchange for a backlink)
> - Generate a sharable report URL (every shared report = a backlink + brand impression)
> - Always include a clear "monitor this domain forever for $39/month" CTA
> - Capture an email before delivering the report

After (add a fifth bullet):

> - Once free-scan volume passes roughly 500 scans/month, start aggregating anonymized results into a quarterly "State of Small Business AI Visibility" post — the same playbook as Profound's Index, scoped down to SMBs instead of enterprise brands. This is a backlink/PR play, not a product feature, and should wait until volume supports a credible sample size.

Rationale: Profound just proved this playbook works as a category-defining PR move. Olokas can't match its scale, but a small-business-scoped version is a cheap, differentiated angle once there's enough scan data to make it credible — and explicitly gated so nobody tries it prematurely on thin data.

## 3. Proposed action — ranked by importance

1. **Add the zero-click stat requirement to Section 5's content cadence** (highest priority, no engineering cost). Immediately upgrades the credibility of every future blog post and pillar page.
2. **Add the "not an execution agent" line to Section 1** (medium priority). Cheap, and keeps the identity section from reading as dated the next time an operator or customer compares Olokas to Profound's newer positioning.
3. **Add the scan-volume-gated "State of Small Business AI Visibility" idea to Section 5** (lowest priority — explicitly deferred until ~500 scans/month). Worth having on record now so it isn't reinvented later, but shouldn't be built before the data supports it.

Out of scope for this proposal, still pending from 2026-05-17: the six-engine expansion and comparison-page reprioritization. Both remain unactioned and still worth doing — this proposal doesn't repeat them, just flags that they haven't been picked up yet. Also out of scope: any pricing change (that's a pricing-experiment proposal, not this one).
